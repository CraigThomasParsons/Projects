# Product Vision

## The Vision Statement
> To create an intuitive and accessible radial menu system that works seamlessly with the Azeron Cyro and Cyborg controllers, utilizing directional keys for effortless navigation.

## Business Goals
Enhance user experience through streamlined menu navigation
- Increase accessibility for users with preferred controller inputs
- Expand compatibility across various games and platforms

## Success Metrics
User adoption and retention rates
- Positive feedback and reviews
- Increased sales and market penetration
