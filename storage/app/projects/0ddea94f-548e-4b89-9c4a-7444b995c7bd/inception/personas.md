# Target Personas

## Dog Owner
**Tech Level:** Medium

### Goals
Find trustworthy, affordable dog walkers

### Frustrations
High cost of existing dog walking services, difficulty finding reliable walkers

### Context
Dog owners use the app at home or in the office to schedule dog walks

---

## Walker
**Tech Level:** Medium

### Goals
Earn extra money by walking dogs, gain experience working with animals

### Frustrations
Difficulty finding consistent, well-paying dog walking opportunities

### Context
Walkers use the app on-the-go to find and accept dog walking jobs
